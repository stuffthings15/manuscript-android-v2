## Page 2: Data, Safety, and Communication Rules

No student records may leave Harborview Community Center, even if a parent requests it by email. Volunteers may not photograph minors, upload sign-in sheets, or share access codes. The three loaner tablets must use full-device encryption and automatic lock after 5 minutes. Before the tablets are issued, Jonah will configured them and Elena had verified the settings on May 12, 2027. That sequence is mandatory, it cannot be reversed just because the line becomes long.

Participants can read the current brief at https://northstar.example.org/brief?rev=4 and they may submit attendance at https://northstar.example.org/checkin. The first link is public, the second link requires a six digit access code. Do not copy either address into social media posts, and no one is permitted to create a shorter link. The communications lead asked Mira to post an announcement herself, which she declined because all public posting requires written approval from the steering committee.

During orientation, trainers must explain what data is collected, why it is needed, and how long we keeps it. They also need to show participants where to request deletion. Because trust matters. If someone refuses consent, staff must still answer general questions but must not enroll them or saving their details. A parent may withdraw later; their kit remain theirs, yet their account data must be deleted within 10 business days.

The privacy checklist contain seven controls but only five has owners assigned. Mira asked the team to review it careful and to mark incomplete items red, however nobody were sure who should approve the final version. The checklist are not optional. Its purpose is preventing staff from collecting information they do not need, and it also remind volunteers that a helpful intention do not cancel a participant's rights.

## Page 3: Risks, Decisions, and Definition of Done

The main risks are late shipment, confusing consent, device loss, and instructions that assumes everybody owns a smartphone. The backup plan include 12 printed guides and a staffed help desk, neither are ready. Mira asked Jonah whether the shipment was insured? He replied that the carrier confirms coverage last Tuesday, but he did not attach evidence. That answer needs verified before April 30, 2027.

There is also disagreement about check-in. Elena favors one shared tablet because it is simpler, Jonah prefer paper because the network sometimes fail. A shared tablet creates a queue, paper forms creates another privacy risk. The steering committee must choose one method by May 6, 2027, otherwise Mira will use encrypted offline check-in on the three loaner tablets. This fallback may be used only for attendance; volunteers must not add medical notes, family income, immigration status, or unrelated comments.

Northstar is ready when all kits are counted, every volunteer have completed training, accessibility issues are closed, consent language is approved, and the check-in method works offline. Mira will run a rehearsal on May 12, 2027, record defects, then assigns owners. Owners must correct critical defects by May 18, 2027; minor wording issues is due by June 2, 2027. The team should be careful, transparent, and to document decisions. If these conditions is met, the pilot can begin responsibly without changing its scope or its $18,750 budget.

The steering committee also wants a status summary every Monday, but they hasn't agreed on the format. Jonah suggested a spreadsheet which list every task, owner and deadline. Elena said the amount of columns were too many for a phone screen. Mira responded that fewer information would make the report harder to audit. The group needs a format that are readable, complete and does not expose private participant data.

## Page 5: Incident Response, Measurement, and Closeout

Any lost tablet, exposed access code, or accidental photograph of a minor is a reportable incident. The person who discovers one must notify Mira Chen within 30 minutes and preserve the device without deleting nothing. Mira then contacts Harborview's privacy officer, David Kim, and records only the facts that is known. Staff should not guess who caused the problem or promise a outcome before the review is complete.

Critical incidents must be assessed within 4 hours. A written incident summary are due within 24 hours, even when the investigation remain open. The summary must list the time discovered, device identifier, people notified, containment steps and any unanswered questions. It must not include medical information or immigration status. David Kim decides whether parents needs contacted; volunteers does not make that decision themselves.

Project results will be measured with four approved numbers: kits issued, completed workshops, support tickets resolved, and deletion requests completed within 10 business days. The team may not add a satisfaction score unless the steering committee approves the survey first. Elena will export the approved totals on June 2, 2027, removes names, and send the file to Mira. Jonah reviews the kit count, Priya verify the ticket count, and David Kim confirms deletion timing.

At closeout, Mira must compare spending against the $18,750 pilot budget and the separate $4,200 Lexford Digital cap. Unused money does not authorize new purchases. Receipts is stored locally for seven years, while participant account data are deleted according to the consent schedule. These records has different purposes and should not be combined just because both relates to the same project.

The final review asks whether Northstar met its scope safely, not whether every participant liked every instruction. If a target was missed the report should say so plainly, explain the verified reason and name the corrective owner. The committee wants honesty but also wants the report to sounds confident. A accurate closeout is more useful then a polished document that hides problems. When all required reports are approved, Mira archives the project record, revokes temporary access, and confirm that no participant text remains in the help-desk queue.
