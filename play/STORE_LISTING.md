# Manuscript store listing

## Main listing

**App name**  
Manuscript

**Slogan**  
Flawless writing powered locally.

**Short description**  
Private grammar and style correction that runs on your device. Nothing uploaded.

**Full description**

Your words never leave your device.

Cloud writing tools upload everything you type to their servers. Manuscript
cannot: its correction AI runs entirely on your phone, it has no ads and no
analytics, and it never sends your text to a Manuscript server. Correct
messages, emails, and documents in places where uploading text is not an
option - client work, confidential drafts, or simply your own private words.

In the Manuscript editor, tap the small icon when text is ready. Review one to
three complete sentences, choose a correction or tone, then replace the text or
dismiss the suggestion. The compact writing card grows only as much as its
content needs. For supported normal writing fields, you can optionally enable
the across-app writing helper. Tap its small icon to open a review panel in the
keyboard area. The panel shows the suggestion without repeating the original
text and returns to your selected keyboard when closed. Android's selected-text
**Process Text** action remains available as a fallback in compatible apps.

Writing tools:

- Proofread
- Improve
- Rephrase
- Shorten
- Friendly
- Formal
- Professional
- Diplomatic
- Empathetic
- Cooperative
- Assertive
- Caveman

Manuscript checks spelling, grammar, punctuation, clarity, and sentence
structure. A selected tone applies to the current text and stays selected for
the next block.

Corrections run on your device. Manuscript has no ads or analytics and does not
send your text to a Manuscript server. The optional across-app helper is off by
default and requires a clear disclosure plus activation in Android
Accessibility settings. It uses a bounded accessibility overlay in the keyboard
area, ignores password fields, and changes text only after you tap **Replace
Text**. It does not request the application-overlay permission or install an
`InputMethodService` keyboard.

The optional AI is downloaded from Hugging Face over HTTPS only after
you approve it. The download request contains no writing. The AI host
receives normal connection data, such as the IP address and requested file.

Voice input comes from your Android keyboard, such as Gboard. Manuscript does
not record or receive audio. Voice-input availability and processing depend on
the keyboard and its settings.

## Required graphics

- App icon: 512 x 512 PNG, up to 1 MB.
- Feature graphic: 1024 x 500 PNG or JPG, up to 15 MB.
- Phone screenshots: at least 2; use 1080 x 1920 or larger.
- Recommended screenshots: accessibility setup, across-app icon, keyboard-area
  review panel, tone and Regenerate controls, Replace Text result, keyboard
  return, and privacy/setup screen.

Do not show private text, account details, device IDs, or test email addresses
in store images.
